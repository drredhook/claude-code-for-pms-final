# 03 · Rewind — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly.

Last session you read four conversations and every support ticket
since 4.2 — and found the two piles did not agree.

At the end of the session, type wrap up, and Claude Code saves the
prompts you wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt — updates
CLAUDE.md, and saves your work to GitHub. By Module 6 this file is a
prompt library built from your own questions.

---

### 1.

Is any population of users driving the change more than others?

### 2.

Yes look at responders for commonalities and differences, and also see if time of day shows anything interesting

### 3.

We know it is very likely a change in 4.2 is causing issues with  both getting and responding to calls. I want to quantify the user impact here in one number - give me three different impact numbers this data shows, with comparative statements around severity of impact and confidence in effects. Briefly describe how these three angles were assembled with traceability back to the data itself

### 4.

Do any considerations change if we look at responders vs handlers?

### 5.

### 6.

Compare the support_tickets table on rook-database against the pings table. Do they agree with each other? When did people start writing in, and when do the numbers actually move?

### 7.

### 8.
